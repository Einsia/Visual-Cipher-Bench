The tables give every inferred intermediate state; none is printed on the puzzle page.

All four observations are needed to distinguish the rule within the tested family. The completed four-contact panel agrees. Its H candidate repeats the output's identities and faces (all three black); Z has a white cross and P a white diamond. The tick at H and the filled H blank establish extraction without explaining the hidden local rule. Because the candidates omit collars, the tags of each question's final state are never shown and must be carried through the simulation.

Track moving cartridge objects, not fixed rail records. At each contact, read the old selected tag, flip its recipient, inspect the partner's updated face, write only the selected tag, then exchange the pair. An earlier write can change a later flip recipient, and an earlier face change can alter a later write. A face-independent list of contacts therefore does not suffice.
