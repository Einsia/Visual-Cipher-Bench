# Word list source

`english_words.txt` (154,114 entries) is the union of the alphabetic headwords of
CMUdict (https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict, BSD-style license, see
`LICENSE-CMUdict.txt`) and the index headwords of Open English WordNet 2025
(https://en-word.net, CC BY 4.0, derived from Princeton WordNet; see
`LICENSE-WordNet.txt` and `LICENSE-WordNet-WNDB.txt`). Entries were uppercased
and deduplicated; no other changes.
