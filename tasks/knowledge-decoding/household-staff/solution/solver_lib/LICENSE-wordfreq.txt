wordfreq word list data

Files in this directory derived from wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq),
version 3.1.1, top_n_list('en', ...), restricted to ASCII alphabetic tokens.

Citation: Robyn Speer (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0. Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437
