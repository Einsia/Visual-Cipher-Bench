# Third-party notices

Bundled reference-solver resources retain their original notices and license texts. This index does not assign a license to the benchmark itself. Fonts, dictionaries, word lists, and vendored solver libraries are used by the reference solutions; they are not added to the agent image.

## Component-specific terms

- **Lost Property.** The bundled `common_words_20k.txt` is derived from wordfreq 3.1.1 and distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its [provenance notice](tasks/knowledge-decoding/lost-property/solution/solver_lib/LICENSE-common-words.txt) records the derivation, hashes, attribution links, and successful offline reference-solver validation. The earlier Google-corpus-derived list is not included.
- **Household Staff.** The derived `english_words.json` follows CC BY-SA 4.0 as documented in the [word list notice](tasks/knowledge-decoding/household-staff/solution/solver_lib/WORDLIST_NOTICE.md). The CMUdict and WordNet material listed there follows its own terms.
- **Echo Vault.** All 110 native libraries and five Python package files match the official OR-Tools 9.15.6755 wheel. The [component notice](tasks/logic-puzzles/echo-vault/solution/solver_lib/vendor/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies the native and embedded dependencies, component-specific licenses, and corresponding source and integration patches. The [upstream license texts](tasks/logic-puzzles/echo-vault/solution/solver_lib/vendor/licenses/README.md) accompany the runtime.
- **Overview artwork.** Model-provider names and logos in [`framework.png`](docs/assets/framework.png) and [`framework.pdf`](docs/assets/framework.pdf) identify the evaluated systems. Rights in those marks remain with their respective owners.
